# The 100-Level Blueprint

Four eras, compressing 4:3:2:1. Level counts shrink while intensity rises;
session lengths run inversely, so total playtime stays roughly balanced.

**Status:** Levels 1–5 are built (`src/levels/first-narrowing.js`). Everything
below level 6 is specification, not implementation.

> **Reproportioning note.** This blueprint was originally written against an
> even 25/25/25/25 split. The move to 40/30/20/10 means later eras shed levels
> and the first absorbs more. The level *concepts* below are sound; their era
> assignments past level 25 need rework. See ROADMAP.md § Open questions.

## The Second Narrowing — Levels 41–70
*Judgment and balance. Soft multi-cycle deadlines appear; still no hard fail.*

Themes: settlement and civic systems. Encoding knowledge without writing.
Acoustic architecture. Resource equilibrium. Scaffolded models built and then
dismantled (**The Frame**). Wealth and energy conservation (**The Balance**).

Representative concepts: the mnemonic vault; the amphitheatre; the grain
standard; the energy debt; the central trust; wealth equilibrium; simple models
for teaching; dismantling those models; the water grid; the trade guilds;
maintaining a settlement across a hundred simulated years.

---

## The Third Narrowing — Levels 71–90
*Urgency. Real-time pressure, real fail states. New engine work required.*

Themes: expansion, systemic toxicity, crisis. **The Weight** (rigidity, deadlock)
and **The Bloom** (cascading feedback where each patch spawns two failures)
appear as named adversary patterns.

Representative concepts: bureaucratic stall; the closed advisory loop;
hyper-inflation; stripping enforcement pillars; the inertial trap; fragmenting a
multi-front crisis into solvable parts; the hydra problem; rapid structural
overhaul; precision inputs under a hard timer; the five depths examined under
duress (Shell, Current, Weather, Lattice, Core); preserving knowledge through
collapse; rebuilding from ash.

---

## The Fourth Narrowing — Levels 91–100
*The extremes of both, back to back. Ten levels, each distinct.*

Themes: complex systems, planetary scale, the limits of perception.

Representative concepts: tracing a corrupted node through an opaque system;
resolving a runaway loop by introducing an unattached observer; filtering true
signal from overwhelming noise; closed-loop ecosystems with zero waste;
encoding knowledge for a receiver with no shared context; eliminating noise
until a fragile state holds; a reaction window of one millisecond; deriving a
single unifying account from two incompatible models; the Seeker turning
**The Filter** on their own assumptions.

**Level 100 — Level Zero.** No trophy, no credits. Return to Level 1, the dark
cave, now as the one who guides the next Seeker. The cycle turns over.
